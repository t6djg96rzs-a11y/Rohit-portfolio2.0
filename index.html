About me

I'm a videographer and editor based in Indore. I plan, shoot and edit videos for brands, store owners and creators: Reels, product videos and brand films.

I shoot on iPhone 13 Pro and Blackmagic Camera, and I'm still learning new tools to push my edits further.

My inspiration began with a fascination for visual storytelling. Exploring edits, music and transitions sparked my creativity. Over time, editing became a passion I wanted to pursue professionally.

Editing inspiration: Casey Neistat.
